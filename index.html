THE DIRECTION

DEATH, REFINED.

DEADS needed an identity that could feel recognizable, ownable and scalable across an expanding ecosystem.

The direction builds around a single architectural symbol — combining the structure of a grave monument with the silhouette of a skull through negative space.

CONCEPT

The DEADS logo combines a grave monument and skull into one bold, recognizable insignia.

The structured silhouette and negative space create a dark yet refined symbol representing death, legacy and permanence.

The result is an architectural mark designed to remain recognizable across digital platforms, products, badges and the wider DEADS ecosystem.
